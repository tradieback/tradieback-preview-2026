1 What we'll deliver

We'll build and hand over the Tradie Launch install — a complete operating platform for your trade business, including:

  • A custom mobile-first website designed around your brand, services, and service area
  • Branded CRM with lead pipeline and customer database
  • Online quoting + invoicing with Stripe card payments
  • Automated email + SMS workflows (booking confirmations, reminders, review requests, quote follow-ups)
  • Auto-text-back on missed calls within 60 seconds
  • Google Business Profile optimisation guidance
  • Domain configuration and sending email setup
  • Ongoing Tradie Care — maintenance, light edits, hosting, and platform support

2 Timeline

The 7-day install clock starts on the day you submit a complete intake form and upload required content (logo + 8–15 job photos) to the Google Drive folder we provide.

We commit to delivering the live site within 7 working days of intake completion. If we miss that through our own delay (not yours), we keep working at no extra cost until the site is live.

3 Pricing — Founder Pilot

Because you're one of our first three Founder Pilot clients, the standard install fee is waived in full. In exchange, you agree to the obligations in section 5 below.

  • Install fee: NZD $0 (normally $3,000–$5,000 + GST)
  • Tradie Care (months 1–3): NZD $0
  • Tradie Care from month 4 onwards: NZD $99/month + GST, billed via Stripe
  • Tradie Leads (optional add-on): NZD $1,500/month + GST. Ad spend paid by you direct to Google and Meta. No markup.

You can cancel ongoing Tradie Care at any time with 14 days' written notice. No exit fees.

4 You own everything

From day one, you own — free and clear:

  • The domain name registered for your business
  • All site code, content, copy, and images created for you
  • All customer data captured through the platform (leads, bookings, invoices, reviews)

If you ever leave, we'll provide a full export of your data and help with handover within 14 days. We retain rights to our underlying platform templates, build methodology, and any reusable modules we've built across clients.

5 Founder Pilot obligations

In exchange for the waived install fee and three months of free Tradie Care, you agree to:

  • Give us honest feedback during the install — the rough version, not the polite one
  • Allow us to use your business name and before/after results in TradieBack's marketing (website, social media, sales conversations)
  • Provide a written review and a short (3–5 minute) video testimonial within 60 days of your site going live
  • Be available for one introduction conversation with another tradie in the following 6 months (no obligation to convert — just an introduction)

6 Your responsibilities

To keep the 7-day clock running, you agree to:

  • Submit the intake form within 7 days of signing this agreement
  • Upload requested content (logo, 8–15 job photos, brand assets) to the Drive folder within 7 days of receiving the link
  • Respond to feedback or content questions within 2 business days during the install
  • Provide accurate business information for the website
  • Maintain valid trade licences and insurance for the trade represented on the site

7 What we don't guarantee

We warrant that the install will be done with reasonable skill, care, and to industry standards. We do not guarantee specific business outcomes (e.g., lead volume, Google ranking position, revenue increase) — those depend on your market, your pricing, your service quality, and factors outside our control.

Our total liability under this agreement is capped at the total fees you've paid us in the 12 months before any claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.

8 Termination

Either party can end this agreement:

  • For convenience, with 30 days' written notice
  • Immediately for material breach not remedied within 14 days of written notice

On termination, you keep all data, code, content, and domain (per section 4). You pay any outstanding fees due up to the termination date. We help with the handover.

9 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep each other's business, financial, and operational information confidential — except for information that becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, information disclosed under legal obligation, or marketing references explicitly permitted under section 5.

10 Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of New Zealand. Any disputes will be resolved in the courts of Wellington, New Zealand. This document is the complete agreement between the parties; any changes must be in writing and signed by both.
