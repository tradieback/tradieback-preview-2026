1 What we deliver

We'll build and hand over the Tradie Launch install — a complete operating platform for your trade business. It includes both the public-facing website and the back-office software that runs it:

The website

  • Custom mobile-first website designed around your brand, services, and service area (no templates)
  • Performance build — sub-2-second load, click-to-call + WhatsApp buttons, hand-coded (no WordPress bloat)
  • SEO foundation — schema markup, title tags + meta descriptions, internal linking, NAP consistency
  • Dedicated service page per service offered (one page per service, not a bullet list)
  • Suburb pages targeting your service area (typically 5+ suburbs)
  • Trust elements — Google reviews pulled live onto the site, real job photo gallery, SSL + privacy + professional details
  • Google Business Profile setup + optimisation (categories, services, photos, posting cadence)

The platform (software + setup)

  • Branded CRM — lead pipeline (New Lead → Quoted → Booked → Done → Paid), customer database, email + SMS + WhatsApp threading per contact, tagging
  • Online booking calendar — synced with your Google or Outlook calendar, buffer times, auto-confirmations + day-before reminders
  • Integration with your existing job-management tool (Tradify, ServiceM8, Fergus, simPRO or similar) — new leads from the website flow into your tool automatically, no double-entry. If you don't currently use a job tool, native quoting + invoicing with Stripe card payments is included instead (recurring invoices, deposits, partial payments, automatic GST, Xero-ready)
  • Booking confirmations + appointment reminders configured
  • Quote follow-up nudges (day 1, 3, 7) — sent through the CRM regardless of where the quote itself lives
  • Domain configuration (DNS, MX, SPF/DKIM) + sending email setup on your own domain
  • Hosting on Netlify (global CDN) — 99.9% uptime, SSL renewed forever, daily backups, security patches. Portable: if you ever leave, the full site code is yours and can be moved to any host of your choosing
  • Walkthrough + handover — you and your team trained on the dashboard before go-live

Active growth automations — auto-text-back on missed calls, review request campaigns, paid ads, ad management, local SEO management beyond setup — are part of Tradie Leads, not Tradie Launch (see section 3). Ongoing Tradie Cover begins the day your site goes live (see section 4 for what's included).

2 Timeline

The 7-day install clock starts on the day you submit a complete intake form and upload required content (logo + 8–15 job photos) to the Google Drive folder we provide. We commit to delivering the live site within 7 working days of intake completion.

If we miss the 7-day deadline through our own delay, we keep working at no extra cost until the site is live. This continuation does not apply where the delay is caused by you — including but not limited to incomplete intake answers, missing photos or logo, slow feedback on drafts (over 2 business days), or unresponsiveness. The 7-day clock pauses whenever we're waiting on you and resumes when we receive what we asked for.

4 What Tradie Cover includes

Tradie Cover is the ongoing monthly plan that keeps your platform online and running. It covers three things:

Included

  • Website hosting — fast global CDN, SSL, domain renewal
  • Website maintenance — security updates, platform updates, light content edits (text, photos, services — up to 30 min/mo), email/WhatsApp support (1 business day response)
  • Software access — CRM, customer database, booking calendar, email + SMS sending platform, lead-sync to your job tool. If you don't use a job tool, native quoting + invoicing + Stripe is included here too

Not included (lives in Tradie Leads)

  • Auto-text-back on missed calls
  • Review request automation
  • Active lead-gen email + SMS campaigns
  • Paid ads (Google + Meta) and ad management
  • Local SEO + Google Business Profile management

Also not included: custom development beyond light edits, full redesigns, third-party software fees (Xero, MYOB, etc.), and Stripe transaction fees (paid by you direct). Light edits over 30 min/mo are charged at NZD $80/hr + GST or rolled into a custom scope. Always quoted before any work starts.

5 You own everything

From day one, you own — free and clear:

  • The domain name registered for your business
  • All site code, content, copy, and images created for you
  • All customer data captured through the platform (leads, bookings, invoices, reviews)

If you ever leave, we'll provide a full export of your data and help with handover within 14 days. We retain rights to our underlying platform templates, build methodology, and any reusable modules we've built across clients.

6 Your responsibilities

To keep the 7-day clock running, you agree to:

  • Submit the intake form within 7 days of signing this agreement
  • Upload requested content (logo, 8–15 job photos, brand assets) to the Drive folder within 7 days of receiving the link
  • Respond to feedback or content questions within 2 business days during the install
  • Provide accurate business information for the website
  • Maintain valid trade licences and insurance for the trade represented on the site

7 What we don't guarantee

We warrant the install is performed with reasonable skill and care to industry standards. We do not guarantee specific business outcomes (lead volume, Google ranking, revenue increase) — those depend on your market, pricing, service quality, and factors outside our control. Our total liability under this agreement is capped at the greater of (a) total fees paid in the preceding 12 months, or (b) NZD $5,000. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.

8 Termination

  • Either party can end this agreement with 30 days' written notice for convenience
  • Either party can end immediately for material breach not remedied within 14 days of written notice

On termination, you keep all data, code, content, and domain (per section 5). You pay any outstanding fees due to the termination date. We help with the handover.
