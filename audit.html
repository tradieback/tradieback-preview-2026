// Fair questions

The straight answers.

Is it actually free?
Yes. No card, no trial that auto-bills. The audit, walkthrough, and written report are yours to keep. If you want us to fix what we find, that's a separate conversation — only if you ask.
What's the catch?
There isn't one. The audit takes us about an hour and lets us prove we know what we're doing. Most tradies want help fixing what we find. Some don't — that's fine.
How long does the whole thing take me?
About 30 minutes total. Two minutes to fill the form, then a 30-min Zoom walkthrough when the report's ready. No logins needed.
Will you try to sell me something on the call?
Only if you ask. We go through every finding, you ask questions, that's it. If you want help fixing things, we'll talk. If not, good luck — we mean it.
What do I need to send you?
Nothing beyond the form. No logins, no access to anything. The audit runs from the outside — exactly how a customer sees you.
