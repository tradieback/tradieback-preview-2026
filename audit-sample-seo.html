Why You're Buried

The specific reasons Google ranks your competitors above you. Each one is fixable — most within 30 days.

01
CRITICAL
Biggest lever

No suburb-level service pages

You have one "Services" page for all of Wellington. Your top competitor has 14 suburb-specific pages — "plumber kelburn", "plumber karori", "plumber island bay", etc. Google treats each one as a separate ranking opportunity. You're competing for one spot; they're competing for 14.

FIX: Build 8–12 suburb pages targeting the patches that pay
02
CRITICAL
Trust signal

Only 4 backlinks from local sites

Google uses backlinks from other trusted local sites as a ranking signal. Wellington Pipe Pros has 47 local backlinks (Yellow Pages, NoCowboys, local business directories, Neighbourly, Trade Me trades). You have 4. That's a 10× gap Google reads as "they matter, you don't".

FIX: Claim all local directory listings · Build out citations
03
HIGH
Compound effect

Zero blog posts or content updates since 2022

Google treats stale sites as dormant. Your competitor publishes 2–4 posts a month on common plumbing problems ("why is my hot water tepid", "how much does a new cylinder cost"). Those posts rank for long-tail searches AND signal to Google that the site is actively maintained. You're losing that ranking signal entirely.

FIX: Weekly content drops · Target common customer questions
04
HIGH
Technical

Missing schema markup + broken sitemap

Your sitemap.xml returns a 404 and you have no LocalBusiness schema. Google has to guess what your business does and where it operates. Your competitor's schema tells Google the exact service areas, business hours, review rating, and accepted payment methods — which is why they get rich results and you don't.

FIX: Generate sitemap · Add LocalBusiness + Service schema
05
MEDIUM
Quick win

Title tags aren't targeted to keywords

Your homepage title is "Capital Plumbing - Home". Google's ranking algorithm heavily weights the title tag. Your competitor's homepage title is "Wellington Plumber | 24/7 Emergency | Wellington Pipe Pros". Same page type, completely different ranking power.

FIX: Rewrite title tags to include location + service
