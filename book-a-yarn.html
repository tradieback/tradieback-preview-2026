Quick answers

What's actually free in the pilot?
The full Tradie Launch install (custom website + branded CRM + invoicing + email workflows + calendar — normally $5,000). Plus 90 days of Tradie Leads running (Google Ads + landing page + lead qualification — normally $1,497/mo). Total value: ~$9,500. You pay $0 for 90 days. You do pay your own Google Ad spend direct to Google ($800-$1,500/mo recommended).
What happens at day 90?
You either convert to the founder rate (locked 12 months) or walk away with your domain, your customer data, and your content. No exit fees. Decision is yours by day 75 to give us 15 days notice either way.
What do I have to do in return for the pilot?
Three things: (1) honest feedback during the 90 days · (2) permission for us to publish your business name + before/after metrics in our marketing · (3) a 5-min video testimonial at the end. That's the trade — we get our case study, you get the platform free.
Are the founder spots also free?
No — founder spots are paid (at the discounted founder rate). Two free pilot spots are open before June 17. Founder spots open June 17 after the pilots validate the platform.
I'm not sure if I'm a fit. Should I still book a yarn?
Yes — that's what the call is for. 30 min, no pitch. We'll figure out together if it's the right time. Selection criteria: NZ or AU, single-trade focus, $300K+ annual revenue, smartphone + WhatsApp daily, comfortable with tech.
