Quick answers

What's the yarn actually for?
Three reasons people book: walking through their free audit, figuring out if TradieBack's a fit for their business, or planning a website/leads project. We follow whatever conversation you bring — no script.
Will you try to sell me something?
No pitch unless you ask. We talk about your business, you ask questions, that's it. If you want to do work together at the end, we'll book a proper scoping call — but only if you ask.
Who's on the call?
Mathieson — the founder. Not a sales rep, not a junior. The person who'd actually build your site is the person you're talking to.
How long does it take?
20 minutes by default. Sometimes it runs to 30 if the conversation's good. We won't keep you longer than you want.
I'm not sure if I'm a fit. Should I still book a yarn?
Yes — that's exactly what the call is for. We'll figure out together. Rough guide: NZ or AU, single-trade focus, solo to small crew, smartphone + WhatsApp daily, comfortable with tech.
