// Under the hood

What each lever does and why it matters.

If you've never done marketing before — or you've been burned by an agency that couldn't explain what they were actually doing — this is for you. Plain English, no jargon.

Tier 1 — non-negotiable · these directly generate calls
Google Business posts VERY HIGH

Google Business Profile is where 60%+ of local tradie calls come from. When someone searches "plumber near me" the map pack shows up first — before any website.

Posting regularly tells Google "this business is active" and pushes you higher. A dead profile gets buried. 4 posts a week is the sweet spot — Google rewards consistency, not volume.

Review automation VERY HIGH

Reviews are the #1 trust signal. A tradie with 80 reviews at 4.8 stars gets called before one with 12 at 5.0.

Tradies never ask — it's awkward. Automating the SMS removes the awkwardness. This single feature can double your review count in 90 days.

Review responses HIGH

Google confirmed responding to reviews is a ranking factor. Customers read responses. A tradie who replies looks professional. One who ignores — especially negative reviews — looks like they don't care.

Speed matters on negatives — a fast, professional response can win you more customers than the negative review loses.

Local SEO VERY HIGH

The foundation everything else sits on. Schema markup, NAP consistency, service area targeting — tells Google "this plumber serves these 10 suburbs."

Without it, nothing ranks. Most competitors have never touched their SEO — even basic work creates a massive gap.

Tier 2 — growth multipliers · these compound over time
Blog content HIGH — COMPOUNDS

Each blog post is a new page on Google. "How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Wellington" — captures customers at the research stage.

After 6 months: 24 ranking pages. After a year: 48. Competitors who don't blog can't catch up.

Suburb / area pages HIGH — FASTEST WIN

A page targeting "electrician in Karori" ranks for that specific search. 10 suburb pages = 10 fishing lines in the water. More pages = more calls.

Social media MEDIUM-HIGH

Doesn't directly generate calls like Google. But it builds social proof (customers check your Facebook), brand awareness (past customers remember you), and referrals (people share posts).

3 posts/week keeps it alive. 5/week keeps it dominant.

Tier 3 — competitive edge · for tradies who want to dominate
Google Ads HIGH — FAST

Phone rings in week 2. But stops when you stop paying. Best for new businesses, emergency trades, or dominating a specific suburb right now. Spend paid direct to Google — typical $800–$1,500/mo recommended for early traction.

Monthly report RETENTION

Doesn't generate calls. But it's the #1 reason clients stay. "Your GBP calls went from 15 to 43" — that tradie doesn't cancel. The one with no visibility cancels at month 3.
