Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 April 2026

TradieBack ("we", "us", "our") provides website, software platform, and marketing services for trade businesses across New Zealand and Australia. We take your privacy seriously and handle your personal information with care.

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. No legal jargon, just straight talk.

1. What information we collect

When you submit a free audit form or contact us, we may collect the following:

  • Your name
  • Business name
  • Website URL
  • Suburb or location
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)

We also automatically collect anonymised usage data through Google Analytics (see Section 3 below).

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Prepare and deliver your free audit report
  • Contact you about your audit results or enquiry
  • Provide the services you have requested (websites, software platform access, marketing, lead generation)
  • Improve our website and the way we communicate

We will never sell your information to third parties. We do not send spam or add you to marketing email lists without your consent.

3. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects anonymised data such as pages visited, time on site, browser type, and general location. This data helps us improve the site experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors.

We also use Google Fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) to display text on the site. When you load a page, your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch the font files. Google may log this request. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate the site:

  • GoHighLevel — processes form submissions, calendar bookings, invoices, and CRM data on our behalf. When you submit an audit form, book a call, or are invoiced as a client, your data is processed through GoHighLevel's servers. GoHighLevel's privacy policy is available at gohighlevel.com/privacy-policy.
  • Stripe — processes payments for invoices issued via GoHighLevel. When you pay an invoice, your card and billing information are processed through Stripe's servers. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymised site analytics as described above.
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery as described above.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties unless required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as we need it to provide our services or as required by law. Audit form submissions are retained for up to 24 months. If you become a client, we retain your information for the duration of our working relationship and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping purposes.

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see Section 6).

6. Contact us about your privacy

If you want to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or if you have any questions about this policy, get in touch:

  • Email: hello@tradieback.com
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp Us

We will respond to privacy requests within 20 working days.

7. NZ Privacy Act 2020

TradieBack operates in compliance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and its thirteen Information Privacy Principles (IPPs). You have the right to access and request correction of any personal information we hold about you.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy request, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

For Australian users, we also respect the principles of the Australian Privacy Act 1988. If you have a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back occasionally.
