// The story

Why TradieBack exists.

Two things became obvious from a decade on NZ construction sites. The platform is the answer to both.

// 01 · The problem

Brilliant tradies, broken back-office.

The best tradies I worked with on site weren't winning more work because they were better tradesmen. They were worse off — too busy, too good to advertise, too tired at 8pm to send a quote.

Their phone rang once a week from word-of-mouth. The Google search results showed five other companies before theirs. Their books were a kitchen-table mess of receipts and unpaid invoices.

// 02 · The agency trap

Marketing agencies don't speak tradie.

Every "marketing solution" I saw a tradie sign up for was the same — $5,000 cookie-cutter website, generic SEO package, six-month lock-in, and a 22-year-old account manager who couldn't tell a sparkie from a chippie.

Three months in, the tradie cancelled, ate the loss, and went back to word-of-mouth. The whole industry was set up to fail them.

// 03 · What I knew

Construction PM discipline. Applied to small business.

Running $50M+ builds taught me one thing — the system runs the project, not the people. When the system is right, mediocre operators ship great work. When it's wrong, the best foreman in the country burns out.

Same with trade businesses. The tradie isn't the bottleneck. The system around them is.

// 04 · The build

One platform. Built once. Rolled out to every tradie.

TradieBack is the system I'd want if I went back to my mate Wayne — third-generation Wellington plumber, $800K/yr, drowning in admin — and told him "let me fix this for you."

Tradie Launch installs the platform in 7 days. Tradie Leads gets the phone ringing. You stay on the tools. The system runs the rest.

// How we work

Six principles. Non-negotiable.

The rules I refuse to break. They're why TradieBack works — and why you'll never see us pivot to "but we also do cafés."

01

NZ + AU tradies. That's it.

No cafés. No lawyers. No "we also do dentists." The platform works because every decision in it was made for one type of business — yours.

02

You own everything.

Your domain, your customer data, your content, your phone number. If we're not pulling our weight after the 6-month minimum — sack us, walk with everything, no exit fees.

03

Plain English. Always.

No "synergies." No "thought leadership." No "let's circle back." Monthly report tells you how many calls came in and how much money came out. Done.

04

Outcomes over technology.

You don't need to know what's running under the hood. You need the phone to ring, the quotes to go out fast, and the cash to land on time. We worry about the rest.

05

You talk to the founder.

No account managers, no junior sales reps, no offshore support tickets. WhatsApp goes straight to my phone. If I can't pick up, you'll hear back the same day.

06

Ship in 7 days. Or it's free.

If we don't have you live within 7 days of contract, your install is on us. No "design rounds," no "feedback cycles." The platform is built. Your name goes on it. Done.
