// Pricing questions

The straight answers.

Can I just take Tradie Launch and not Leads?
Yep. Launch is a one-off install — when it's done, it's done. Hosting + maintenance is included for 12 months. You can add Leads later (at whatever the going rate is when you sign up).
Can I just take Leads without Launch?
Yes — if your existing site is decent. We'll do a free audit before we start to make sure your site converts well enough to justify ad spend. If it doesn't, we'll tell you straight — and recommend Launch first. Running ads to a broken site is just expensive.
What happens after my 12-month founder lock ends?
Three options: auto-renew at the public rate ($1,497/mo), prepay 12 months annually for a 15% discount, or prepay 24 months for a 25% discount and lock another 2 years. Most founders pick one of the prepay options — locked-in clients get rolled-over founder benefits.
Is the founder rate really limited to 4 spots?
Yes. Real scarcity, not manufactured. The pilot is one client (free, 90 days, in exchange for case study). The 4 founder spots open after the pilot validates. Once those 4 are filled, the founder rate disappears for good — public rate from there forever.
What's the lock-in?
Launch is one-off — no lock at all. Leads has a 6-month minimum so we can actually build the ranking signals (SEO and Google Business take time to compound). After 6 months, sack us anytime with 30 days notice. No exit fees, you walk with everything.
Who pays for the Google Ads spend?
You do — direct to Google. We don't mark it up or hide it. Recommended starting spend is $800–$1,500/mo depending on your trade and city. You control the budget completely. We just manage the campaigns.
Are there setup fees on top?
No. Tradie Launch is the one-off cost (whether founder or public). Tradie Leads has no setup fee. The only spend on top is your Google Ads budget, paid direct to Google. No hidden charges, no "discovery call" fees, no asset transfer fees.
