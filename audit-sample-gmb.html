What's Killing Your Map Pack Position

Every one of these is free to fix and takes less than an hour. Most tradies ignore them because nobody's told them they matter.

01
CRITICAL
Biggest lever

No posts since August 2024

Google treats profiles that haven't posted in 3+ months as dormant and deprioritises them in the map pack. Your competitor posts 3–4 times a week (jobs done, offers, photos from site, FAQs). This alone would move you 3–5 positions up the map pack in 60 days.

FIX: Post 2–4× per week · photos, offers, job updates, FAQs
02
CRITICAL
Trust signal

47 reviews vs 284 — and you're not asking

Google's algorithm weights review count AND velocity. Your top competitor adds ~9 new reviews a month; you add 0–1. They're not getting lucky — they have an automated review request system that triggers the moment a job is marked complete in their job-management software. You have no request system at all.

FIX: Automated review requests after every closed job
03
CRITICAL
Easy win

Only 3 photos uploaded · competitor has 67

Photos are one of the strongest visual ranking signals in the map pack. Google literally shows them above your business name in search results. Your 3 photos are a generic logo, a stock photo of a pipe, and a team photo from 2021. Your competitor has photos of every job type, every team member, and before/after shots of recent work.

FIX: Upload 30+ photos from recent jobs · all service categories
04
HIGH
Hidden damage

12% review reply rate · last reply Nov 2024

Not replying to reviews — especially negative ones — signals to Google that you don't actively manage the profile. You have 3 unanswered 2-star reviews from late 2024 that are still visible to every potential customer who searches for you. Each one is a silent pitch for the competition.

FIX: Reply to every review within 24 hours · draft responses ready
05
HIGH
Q&A gap

4 customer questions · 0 answered

Your profile has 4 unanswered questions from potential customers — "do you do emergency callouts", "what areas do you cover", "do you accept Afterpay", "are you licensed". Google lets anyone answer these if you don't — which means a random stranger could have misinformed a would-be customer. Your competitor answered all 18 of their Q&A entries.

FIX: Answer all open questions · pre-populate common Q&A
06
MEDIUM
Services list

Only 6 services listed · competitor has 18

The Services section of your GBP is another ranking signal. You've listed 6 generic categories ("plumbing", "gas fitting"). Your competitor lists 18 specific services ("hot water cylinder replacement", "emergency blocked drain", "gas hot water install") — which means they rank for 18× more specific searches than you.

FIX: Add 12+ specific services · use exact customer language
