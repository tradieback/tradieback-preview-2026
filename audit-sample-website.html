Top 5 Issues Costing You Jobs

Ranked by estimated impact on your phone ringing. Each one has a severity tag and an estimated cost in lost jobs per month.

01
CRITICAL
~3 jobs/mo

Site takes 8.2 seconds to load on mobile

Your site is loading 4× slower than Google's "good" threshold for mobile. On a 4G connection, a tradie's customer will bounce before the homepage even finishes rendering. Google Lighthouse flags 11 unused scripts and 6 render-blocking resources. Fixing this alone would recover an estimated 3 jobs a month.

FIX: Remove unused scripts · Optimise images · Implement lazy loading
02
CRITICAL
~2 jobs/mo

No click-to-call button on mobile

Your phone number is displayed as plain text, not a tel: link. Mobile visitors can't tap to call — they have to manually type it. 60% of mobile visitors to trade websites convert via click-to-call. You're leaving that entire segment on the table.

FIX: Wrap all phone numbers in tel: links · Add sticky mobile call bar
03
HIGH
~1 job/mo

No Google reviews displayed on the homepage

You have 47 Google reviews with a 4.7 rating, but none of them are visible anywhere on the site. A visitor lands, sees no reviews, and assumes you're a one-man-band with no track record — even though the opposite is true.

FIX: Embed Google review widget above the fold · Show star rating + review count
04
HIGH
~1 job/mo

"Get a Quote" button is below the fold

Your primary conversion CTA requires scrolling to find. Above-the-fold CTAs convert 3.2× better than below-the-fold ones. Also, the button is grey — low-contrast buttons on trade sites underperform high-contrast (orange/red) ones by ~40%.

FIX: Move primary CTA above the fold · Change to high-contrast colour
05
MEDIUM
~0.5 job/mo

No schema markup or sitemap.xml

Google has a harder time understanding what your business does and where it operates. This hurts your SEO ceiling (see Part 02) and means Google can't show rich results like business hours, service areas, or review stars in search.

FIX: Add LocalBusiness schema · Generate sitemap.xml · Submit to Search Console
