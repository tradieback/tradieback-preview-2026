TradieBack — the operating platform for NZ + AU tradies

Built by someone who's spent nearly a decade on construction sites. The mission: keep great tradies on the tools by running the digital side for them.

What it is

TradieBack is a done-for-you operating platform for trade businesses. We hand a tradie a complete website + back-office system + lead generation engine — built, configured, and running — in 7 days. They stay on the tools. We run everything else.

Service 01 · Live

Tradie Launch

The 7-day install. Custom website + CRM + invoicing + automated reviews/quotes/payments — all bundled, all built for tradies. Replaces ~$12K/year of disconnected software + agency fees.

From $5K install · $99/mo

Tradie Leads

The growth engine. Google + Meta ads, local SEO, Google Business management, review automation, lead qualification. Runs once Launch is live. First qualified lead in 30 days or month's free.

From $799/mo · ad spend direct to Google

The problem — great on the tools, drowning everywhere else

Tradies are world-class at their craft. They're also the worst-served small business segment when it comes to digital. The same tradie who can rebuild a hot water cylinder blindfolded can't get past page 3 on Google — because nobody's ever taught them, and the agencies that try don't speak their language.

My runway — building it the right way, not the fast way

I'm not putting all my eggs in one basket. TradieBack is a serious build with a long horizon — but I refuse to be on the computer 24/7 and refuse to burn out before clients arrive. The plan is to build it in parallel with the right day job, in the right network.

The networking move

Mid 2026

Heading to Australia for an extended trip — networking inside the NZ + AU construction industry, getting deeper into the construction skills + standards on both sides of the Tasman. Every conversation is a potential client, partner, or referral.

Likely landing in a PM role in either Sydney/Brisbane or Christchurch. The PM income pays the bills while TradieBack scales. The role itself puts me in front of 30+ subcontractor trade businesses every week — exactly the people I'm building this for.

The PM role isn't a backup plan — it's the network. My first 10–20 clients are going to come from sites I'm on, conversations at smoko, sparkies I've worked with for years. When TradieBack revenue covers my drawings, the PM role ends.

Why not full-time on TradieBack now?

Honest answer

Two reasons. First — I can't be on the computer all day. Construction-fit body, construction-fit brain. I do my best thinking on site, not at a desk. The PM role keeps me in the world I'm building for.

Second — I refuse to bootstrap with desperation. Tradies smell desperation in 5 seconds. Building this on a steady income means I can hold standards, say no to bad-fit clients, and grow on signal — not on pressure to make next month's rent.
